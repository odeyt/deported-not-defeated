Every entry is derived from a page that already exists and was researched. The
purpose is distribution of work already done — not generating new claims on
camera, where sourcing is harder to show and mistakes are harder to correct.

---

## Rules

- **Never state visa, legal, or immigration rules on camera without the source
  on screen.** These are YMYL topics and video strips away the citations a page
  can carry.
- **Link to the page, not the affiliate.** Video descriptions send viewers to
  the resource; the page handles disclosure and provider routing.
- Affiliate mentions follow the same approval rule as the site: if it is not
  approved, it is not promoted.
- Do not repurpose a page whose facts have not been verified.

## Why this order

The first two map to the two pages with the clearest commercial intent and the
only monetized category with verified corridor data. Numbers 3, 5, 6, and 9
carry no commercial intent at all and exist to build trust and search presence —
which is the actual bottleneck.

**Do not make #10 until Remitly or a cash-pickup provider is approved.** The
honest answer to "which fits you" is often cash pickup, and we currently earn
nothing on it. Making that video first would mean either steering people wrong
or advertising a competitor's product for free.

---

## Distribution note

YouTube is a search engine with its own index. A video ranking for "how to send
money to Mexico" builds the traffic history Travelpayouts is asking for, and it
does so on a surface Google does not gate behind three months of stable
sessions.

That said: **one good video beats ten thin ones**, exactly as with pages. The
failure mode is identical.
