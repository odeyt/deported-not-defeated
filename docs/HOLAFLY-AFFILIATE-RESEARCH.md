- Holafly has a public affiliate program that says affiliates earn commission on each sale through affiliate discount-coupon attribution.[1]

- Holafly is relevant to travel/arrival connectivity content, especially for visiting family. This does not establish suitability for resident mobile-service needs, publisher acceptance, or expected earnings.
