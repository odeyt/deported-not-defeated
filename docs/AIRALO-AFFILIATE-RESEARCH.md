- Airalo publicly operates an affiliate program through Impact.

- Airalo's public catalogue supported customer eSIM availability for Mexico, Guatemala, El Salvador, Cambodia, Laos, and the Philippines in the completed review.

### Inferences

- DeportedNotDefeated's practical arrival, connectivity, and family-travel content appears aligned with Airalo's publicly described publisher categories.
- Airalo could be useful to the intended audience when an eSIM is compatible with the user's device and trip needs.

### Recommendations

- Retain the existing `airalo` identity and `/go/airalo` route.
- Keep Airalo non-monetized and internally unverified until an operator confirms any real application/account status and obtains an approved tracking URL from Impact.
- Before any form entry, review the exact Impact campaign terms and confirm all traffic, disclosure, trademark, paid-search, email, privacy, and publisher-eligibility rules.

- The public Impact campaign flow required JavaScript; no further navigation was performed because proceeding could lead to login or account creation.
- No official public source in this review established cookie duration, publisher-country eligibility, payout method, reversal, or termination terms.
- Older local fixed-commission wording and specific payment-method claims are not supported by this current official evidence; the supported commission language is "up to 10%."
