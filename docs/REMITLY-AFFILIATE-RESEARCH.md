## Findings

### Verified facts

- Remitly has a public Partner Program managed through Impact.[1]
- The official program page says the program is for individuals and businesses with relevant audiences; a website is not required, and content platforms are named as examples.[1]
- Remitly publicly states a USD $5–$20 commission range per referral depending on send corridor, with 30-day click attribution.[1]
- Remitly publicly states that it supports promotion in active send countries and lists the United States among the program countries.[1]
- The official US-to-Mexico page documents cash pickup, bank deposit, and mobile-wallet delivery options.[3]

### Inferences

- Remitly is a high-priority application candidate because official program evidence and a documented US-to-Mexico cash-pickup-relevant corridor align with the audience need. This is not proof of approval or expected revenue.

### Recommendations

- Preserve the existing `remitly` identity and `/go/remitly` slug. Keep it non-monetized unless an operator receives approval and separately authorizes private tracking-link handling.
- Before any application action, obtain narrow approval for the exact Impact URL and allowed non-sensitive fields. Review the displayed terms before accepting anything.
- Do not use coupon, incentive, paid-search, trademark, email, messaging, social, or privacy claims until official terms are reviewed.

- No official public affiliate terms were reviewed for paid search, trademarks, coupons, incentives, email, messaging, social, privacy, disclosure wording, reversals, clawbacks, or termination.
