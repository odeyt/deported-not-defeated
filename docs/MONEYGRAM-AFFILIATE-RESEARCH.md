## Findings

### Verified facts

- MoneyGram publishes official US corridor pages for Mexico, Guatemala, El Salvador, Cambodia, Laos, and the Philippines; the cited evidence table identifies each supporting source.
- The Mexico corridor documents a substantial cash-pickup-relevant network and multiple recipient methods.[1]
- Every reviewed corridor states that availability can depend on destination, pay-in/pay-out method, recipient bank, and other eligibility factors; see the cited country entries above.

### Inferences

- MoneyGram is service-relevant to all six assessed audience countries, with particularly strong documented US→Mexico cash-pickup relevance. This does not indicate an affiliate relationship or approval.

### Recommendations

- Preserve `moneygram` and `/go/moneygram` as non-monetized until a legitimate program and approval are independently verified.
- Do not add a network, commission, cookie, or application URL from directories, historic listings, search results, or seeds.
- If operator approval is later sought, first perform a fresh public program search; do not contact or access accounts without separate authorization.

### Blocked, unavailable, or contradictory evidence

- No current official MoneyGram affiliate-program, network campaign, terms, commission, cookie, application, traffic, publisher-eligibility, or promotion-restriction source was verified within the six-page official-public review.
- Some MoneyGram corridor retrievals presented a verification/CAPTCHA component after public page content. No bypass, interaction, login, or retry strategy was attempted.
- Public corridor pages include customer sign-up links. They are customer-transfer routes, not affiliate application URLs and were not opened.
